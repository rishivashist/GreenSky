Load Quality Score

Score a load before you book it. Set the price with the Quote Calculator.

How to use it

  1. Gather the factsThe customer's price, a fresh carrier quote, loaded miles and the pickup date.
  2. Fill the five sectionsTop to bottom, about a minute. Each field explains what to enter.
  3. Read the gradeA or B: book it. C: fix first. D: pass. X: do not book.
  4. Work the fixesChange what you can (price, credit, carrier, extras), then watch the score update.
  5. Note the gradeAdd the grade to the load notes in LoadStop for the Friday review.

How the score works

Grades

  • A 80 to 100: book it.
  • B 65 to 79: book it; clear flags if you can.
  • C 50 to 64: fix the flagged items first.
  • D under 50: pass.
  • X deal-breaker: do not book.

Points (out of 100)

  • Price vs floor: 25
  • Customer credit: 15
  • Distance, lane and carrier, load size, lead time, facilities, network fit: 10 each
  • Deductions for risky customer types and freight

Hard rules

  • A price below floor caps the grade at C.
  • Failed credit, a carrier warning sign, or a bill under carrier cost plus $100 means X.
  • Floor = 10% margin and 80% of the minimum net; target = 12% and the full minimum.

Minimum net by distance

  • Under 50 mi: $150
  • 50 to 250 mi: $175
  • 250 to 600 mi: $200
  • 600 to 1,200 mi: $300
  • Over 1,200 mi: $450
Weights reflect what drove profit and losses in Parakeet's loads from January 2024 to November 2026. Review them monthly against actual results.
